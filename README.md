*Reach Community*

Reach Community is a fast, accessible, web-based platform designed to connect individuals with essential local, free, or low-cost community resources,without requiring account creation, login credentials, or burdensome forms.
Whether individuals need immediate assistance with food, housing, healthcare, or childcare, Reach Community provides a direct path to vetted local support.
Disclaimer: This project is an independent demo platform and is not officially affiliated with LA County or 211 services.

*Key Features*

Barrier-Free Design: Immediate access to resources with zero registration, login, or form requirements.

Quick Needs Questionnaire: An interactive, light check-in tool to help match users with resources based on their current situation.
Category Search & Directory: Easily filter and browse services across core needs, including:
Food & Nutrition Assistance
Housing & Shelter
Income & Financial Support
Childcare & Family Services
Healthcare & Medical Care
Urgent Help Bar: Rapid, single-click access to critical crisis hotlines and immediate relief services.
Local Bookmarks: Save important resources directly to the browser/device for offline reference.
Multilingual Support: One-tap toggle between English and Spanish to ensure broad community accessibility.

*Tech Stack*

Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+)
